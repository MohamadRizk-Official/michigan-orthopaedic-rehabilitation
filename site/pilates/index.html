Good to know

Before your first class.

  • Sessions are 55 minutes; group classes are capped at 6 people.
  • No prescription required. The studio is separate from the therapy clinic.
  • 24-hour cancellation policy. A late cancel uses the session credit.
  • Credit and debit cards (no Amex), cash and checks. No insurance or HSA.
  • Wear non-restrictive clothing. Socks are required; grip socks recommended.
  • Athletic shoes for personal training sessions.
