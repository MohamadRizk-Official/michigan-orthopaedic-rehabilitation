  • A specialized pressure tourniquet on the exercising limb reduces arterial inflow and venous outflow, so low-intensity exercise produces the strength and muscle gains normally associated with much heavier loads.

    When it helps

    • Pain reduction
    • Managing load around pain
    • Post-surgical pain relief
    • Restoring range of motion
    • Limiting swelling

    The evidence

    There is strong evidence for gains in strength, muscle hypertrophy and angiogenesis with Blood Flow Restriction Training.
